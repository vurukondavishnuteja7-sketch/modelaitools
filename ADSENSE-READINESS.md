# AdSense readiness checklist for MODEL AI

This project prepares clean ad placement space and basic site transparency; it cannot guarantee Google AdSense approval.

## Before applying
- Confirm the domain and site ownership in your AdSense account.
- Make sure every public tool works as described and has useful, original instructions, examples, limitations and FAQs—not just a blank form.
- Review About, Contact, Privacy Policy, Terms and Disclaimer. Replace `hello@modelaitools.online` if it is not an inbox you control, and keep the policy accurate to the providers and advertising services actually used.
- Test navigation, mobile layout, page load performance, 404s, login state, and tool outputs.
- Keep the ad area labelled and separate from tool buttons, navigation, download links and interactive controls. Do not add ads to login/account/private-message views or create pages just to show ads.
- Do not insert an AdSense loader or display test ads until the site is approved and the correct account code is supplied.
- After approval, replace the ad placeholder only with the official AdSense code assigned to your account. Add the exact `google.com, pub-...` line supplied by AdSense to `/ads.txt`; do not use an example/fake publisher ID.
- If your visitors are in regions where consent is legally required, configure a suitable consent-management solution before personalised advertising.
