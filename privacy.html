Privacy Policy

This page is a starter privacy policy. The site owner must review it and adapt it to the actual tools, analytics, advertising and AI providers enabled on the site before launch.

Information you enter into tools

Many browser-based tools process data locally within your browser. For these tools, the site is designed not to send the tool input to our server. Some functionality can load third-party libraries from a content delivery network.

AI features and uploaded images

AI chat, image analysis, translation, question generation and other AI workflows are available only after the site owner configures a provider. When enabled, submitted text or images may be sent to that provider to fulfil the request. Review the provider’s data retention and privacy terms. Do not submit sensitive or confidential data.

Cookies, analytics and advertising

If analytics, advertising or other third-party services are added, they may use cookies or similar technologies as described by their own policies. Update this page with the actual providers and consent practices used by the site.

Children and student users

Use the tools responsibly. Do not submit personal details or another person’s private information without permission. Site owners should comply with applicable privacy and child-safety requirements.

If advertising is enabled on this website, third-party vendors including Google may use cookies or similar technologies to serve, measure, and improve ads according to their own policies and your settings. Before enabling ads, the site owner must update this policy to accurately describe the advertising products used, provide any required privacy/consent controls, and ensure the site complies with applicable laws and publisher policies. Advertising is not currently represented as active by this starter template.
