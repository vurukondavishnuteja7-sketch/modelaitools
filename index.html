What is MODEL AI?

MODEL AI is an organised collection of practical online utilities for study, content creation, file handling and development. The goal is to make everyday tasks quicker without hiding how each feature works.

Browser tools and AI tools are different

Calculators, case conversion, JSON validation and several image utilities can work in your browser. Features such as AI chat, translation, quiz generation and AI coding assistance require a server-side connection to an AI provider. Those features must not claim to work until the connection is configured and tested.

Responsible use

Always review results before using them for school, business, legal or other important decisions. Do not upload confidential information to a third-party AI provider unless you have reviewed its privacy and data handling terms.
